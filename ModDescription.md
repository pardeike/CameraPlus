Camera+ gives RimWorld a better camera for both close detail and wide map control.

Zoom further out when you need the whole battlefield, zoom further in when you want to inspect the small stuff, and tune how movement feels at both ends.

What it adds:

- extended zoom in and zoom out
- smoother camera movement and edge scrolling
- far-zoom labels and marker controls
- dots, silhouettes, edge markers, and custom marker rules
- keyboard shortcuts for saving and loading views

It is built for players who want to read the map faster without losing the colony details that matter.
